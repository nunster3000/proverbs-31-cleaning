Website hosting

This website is hosted on Vercel. Requests to the website may produce technical hosting logs used to operate and troubleshoot the site. Connected account, booking, and media services use Supabase.

Online accounts and walkthroughs

When online services become available, verified email sign-in will protect your account. Booking details and walkthrough media will be stored in the company’s private backend, with access limited to your account and authorized administrators.

Private guest booking links

When guest access becomes available, a private, one-use link will open a single booking without an account. Links expire after 30 minutes; browser sessions last up to eight hours. The server stores hashed access secrets and uses an HttpOnly session cookie. Close guest access on shared devices and ask Gabrielle for a new link if needed.

Home-access authorization

When online signing is available, your authorization record will include your typed name, account or private guest-access reference, the permission form version, the visit address and schedule, your acknowledgements, and the time signed. Only you and the business administrator can access the signed booking record. A signed copy can be downloaded from your booking.

When uploads are enabled, HEIC/HEIF photos are converted to JPG on your device before upload. Avoid including people, IDs, payment details, or access codes in walkthrough photos and videos.

Online card payments are not currently available. Do not send payment card details through email, cleaning notes, or walkthrough uploads.

For questions about information you provide directly to the company, contact Proverbs 31 Cleaning Co. Before customer registration opens, this notice will be updated with the company’s approved record-retention and deletion practices.
