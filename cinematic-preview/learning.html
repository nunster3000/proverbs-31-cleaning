From preparing a guest space to planning a new service business, the program connects the work of cleaning with the decisions that support it.

01 / HOSPITALITY

Hotel & property teams

Explore a common language for routine care, detailed cleaning and room-by-room work. A starting point for conversations about consistency across your team.

02 / SHORT-TERM RENTALS

Airbnb owners & hosts

Understand cleaning scope, recognize when a space needs deeper attention and communicate expectations more clearly with the people caring for your property.

03 / ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Aspiring business owners

Connect foundational cleaning techniques with choosing services, estimating work, finding clients and building a thoughtful customer experience.

The syllabus / 120 minutes

From first principles
to practical next steps.

One connected introductory class, organized into four modules. Open each module to explore the topics and its practical focus.

01BUSINESS FOUNDATIONSHow to start a cleaning business30 min
  • Choosing your services and ideal clients
  • Essential supplies and equipment
  • How to price cleaning jobs
  • Finding clients and providing good customer service

Practical focusConnect the services you want to offer with the people you want to serve, the tools you need and the work a price must cover.

02ROUTINE CAREBasic cleaning30 min
  • Dusting and wiping surfaces
  • Cleaning kitchens and bathrooms
  • Vacuuming and mopping floors
  • Following a room-by-room checklist
  • Working efficiently without missing details

Practical focusThink through a repeatable room-by-room sequence that balances efficiency with attention to detail.

03DETAILED CAREDeep cleaning40 min
  • Basic cleaning versus deep cleaning
  • Detail work on bathrooms and kitchens
  • Baseboards, buildup, fixtures and hard-to-reach areas
  • Recognizing extra labor and pricing it appropriately
  • Demonstrating proper tools and product use

Practical focusRecognize the difference between routine upkeep and more intensive work, and explain how that difference affects scope and pricing.

04REFLECTION & APPLICATIONQuestions and next steps20 min
  • Review the cleaning checklist
  • Discuss common beginner mistakes
  • Ask questions and explore how to get started

Practical focusBring the modules together and identify your next steps as a host, team member or future business owner.

Learning objectives

Knowledge you can
put to work.

This introductory program is designed to help participants:

  1. Describe the difference between basic and deep cleaning.
  2. Outline a room-by-room cleaning workflow.
  3. Identify essential supplies and appropriate tool use.
  4. Recognize how scope and extra labor inform a cleaning price.
  5. Explain the first steps in defining a service and serving clients.
